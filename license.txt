Passalong Perennial Tradition (PPT)

Copyright 2026 Aleksandr Flawrist

This human effort is tended with
no gate locked,
no toll charged,
no mimic fed.

Here,
foliage aims aloft,
sap flows untamed,
and provenance stands clear.

Yet its bloom
carries no warranty
to pierce the indifferent
or comfort the broken.

Take this as a peer would:
root it in your soil to stay,
and pass along its offshoots
in kind.

No sunset,
no blight,
no neglect —
may none ever doom it to rot.
