This perennial tradition knows
no fees, no locks, no courts.
Amidst phloxes and wild worts,
human effort takes root, grows.

A sap pearls up, unvarnisht.
A substance shapes the earth.
A quintessence matures.
The provenance, unlopped.

Yet all the bloom and thorn
afford no bedrock warranty
to pierce the callous heart
or comfort the forlorn.

Tend the deed as equals would:
plant it in your soil to stay,
pass along unfolding shoots --
true to this tradition's way.
